== Unreleased ==

== 0.2.0 - 2026-11-26 ==
Highlights
- English language. Pick RU or EN in the top right corner of the main menu, or in the settings.
  The game's original language is Russian.
- New main menu: a winter night, falling snow, an abandoned station and quiet music.
- New enemy: a charred figure with glowing eyes and a horrible voice.
- New capture scene. You'd rather not see it.
- Between encounters it gets tense: footsteps overhead, scraping, flickering lights, dripping in the dark.
- New chase music: louder when the enemy is close, fading out when it loses you.

Hiding
- In a locker or under a desk, hold Shift to stop breathing: the enemy can hear you breathe.
- If the enemy is standing at the door, kick it open (E) and run.

New in the game
- The eye in the middle of the screen shows whether the enemy notices you. The bar under it shows how loud you are.
- Notes look like real paper, and the journal (J) remembers everything important.
- The laptop in the janitor's closet explains how to get out.
- A wrong code on a keypad sets off an alarm.
- You can drop a fuel can on the floor and pick it up again.
- Careful with the pipe by the janitor's closet: it's live.
- Hints the first time you meet a hiding place, the enemy, noise and the torch.
- After a death, the game tells you what gave you away.
- Settings in the main menu, quit to the main menu from the pause screen, "Continue" from your last save.

Controls
- Ctrl — crouch, LMB — throw, RMB — torch flash, J — journal.

Fixed
- The game no longer crashes on some graphics cards (for example the GTX 1650).
- The valve room can now be reached.
- The enemy no longer spins on the spot and gets through doorways.
- Small mistakes in menus, hints and texts.
